What it does

Pantry

Everything you have, in one list

Add what you buy with its expiry date, where it lives — fridge, freezer or pantry — and how much of it there is. Filter by what needs attention first.

Reminders

One message a day, not twenty

A single daily digest at the time you choose, covering everything due soon. No per-item pinging, and nothing nags you about food that is already past its date.

Shopping list

From the bin back to the basket

Send something straight to the shopping list when it runs out. Tick it off at the shop and it comes back into the pantry with a fresh date.

Insights

What you actually waste

Which foods you keep binning, how much of what you buy gets used, how long a clean run has lasted. Built only from what you recorded — never from invented numbers.

Yours alone

Offline, and no account

There is no sign-up, no server and no cloud sync. Your food records live in a database on your phone and nowhere else, and every screen works in aeroplane mode. Export a backup whenever you want a copy.

How it works

The loop

Add it · Watch it · Use or bin · Buy again

  1. Add what you boughtName, date, where it is stored. A photo if you want one.
  2. Home tells you what to useSorted by what runs out first, not by what you added first.
  3. A reminder arrives dailyAt your time, on the days before expiry you picked.
  4. Mark it used or binnedSwipe right for used, and the record leaves the pantry.
  5. Send it to the shopping listSwipe left for anything worth buying again.
  6. Shop, then add it backTicking an item off puts it straight back in the pantry.
  7. Check Insights now and thenSee what really happened, and buy differently next time.

Privacy, in one tile

The short version

Your food data never leaves your phone

No account, no server, no sync. Ads from Google AdMob keep the app free and can be switched off in Settings — and your food records, shopping list and history are never shared with the ads SDK or with any advertiser. The full text is on the privacy policy page.

Food safety

Dates, not verdicts

FreshTrack tracks the dates you enter. It cannot tell you whether food is safe to eat. Follow the storage and use-by instructions on the packaging and your local food-safety guidance. When in doubt, throw it out.
