Privacy Policy

FreshTrack — Food Expiry & Waste Saver · Effective date: 29 October 2026

FreshTrack is an offline household food-tracking app published by Ahsanur Soft ("we", "us"). This policy explains exactly what happens to your information.

1. We do not collect your food data

FreshTrack has no account system, no server and no cloud sync. Every food record, shopping list entry and history item you create is stored in a database on your own device. It is never transmitted to us or to anyone else.

There is no sign-up, and you are never asked for an email address, phone number, name or password.

2. Your data stays until you remove it

Your data is removed when you use Settings → Clear all data, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. Because nothing is stored elsewhere, we cannot restore it for you — use Settings → Backup and restore to export a copy first if you want to keep it.

3. Backups

Exporting a backup writes a JSON file to your device. FreshTrack never uploads it. If you choose to share that file to email, a messaging app or a cloud drive, it is then handled by that app under its own terms.

4. Notifications

Expiry reminders are scheduled locally by Android using the dates you enter. Nothing about them leaves your device. You can turn them off at any time in Settings, or in Android's own notification settings for the app.

5. Advertising

FreshTrack shows ads from Google AdMob so it can stay free. To serve and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process information such as:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID);
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (model, OS version, app version, language);
  • ad interactions such as impressions and clicks, and diagnostic data.

This data is collected and processed by Google as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your food records, shopping list and usage history are never shared with the ads SDK, with Google or with any advertiser. You can turn ads off in Settings, and every feature of the app keeps working with no internet connection at all.

Your choices. Where the law requires it — in the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and similar — FreshTrack shows Google's consent form before any ad is requested, and no ad is requested until you have answered it. You can reopen that choice at any time from Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in your Android device settings.

6. Permissions

  • Notifications — to show expiry reminders.
  • Alarms and reminders — optional. If you grant it, reminders arrive at the time you chose; if you do not, Android delivers them a little later and everything else works the same.
  • Run at startup — to restore your pending reminders after a reboot.
  • Internet and network state — used only by the advertising SDK.
  • Photos — only when you choose to attach a picture to a food record. The picture stays on your device.

7. Children

FreshTrack is a general-purpose household utility. It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

8. Food safety

FreshTrack tracks the dates you enter. It cannot tell you whether food is safe to eat. Always follow the storage and use-by instructions on the packaging and your local food-safety guidance. When in doubt, throw it out.

9. Changes

If the way FreshTrack handles data ever changes, this policy and the in-app "Privacy and food safety" screen will be updated before that change ships, with a new effective date on this page.
